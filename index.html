ZipDrop

You get a ZIP code. Drop a pin where you think it is. Closer pins score more, up to 5,000 points a round.

How scoring works
  • Within 5 miles is a perfect 5,000.
  • Points fall off with distance: about 3,600 at 100 mi and 700 at 600 mi.
  • Revealing the state halves that round's points.
  • Tip: the first digit of a ZIP is the region. 0 is New England, 9 is the West Coast.
